# DARK-1: start here

One page. Everything you need in the first ten minutes.

## What it does
Switch on and a dark piece plays by itself: drums, bass, pad, an acid line and a pluck line. You steer it. You do not have to play a note.

## The six things to know
| Press | What happens |
|---|---|
| **PLAY** | Stop and start. |
| **PRESETS knob** | The next piece of music. |
| **GLO + PRESETS** | The next genre (21 in all). |
| **SELECT dial** | The next page. |
| **HOME** | Back to the first page. Press it whenever you feel lost. |
| **Hold any button for one second** | A help card says what that button does. |
| **Tap SEL** | Opens the list of every shortcut. Turn SELECT for the next page. SEL, HOME or any button closes it. |
| **Hold HOME** | Settings. Knob 2 is **Advanced**: turn it On to show every page. |

## Play on top of it
Go to the **JAM** page (turn SELECT until the tab says JAM). The keys now play only notes that fit the music.

- **JAM:** the keys, the octave, Answer. The arp and Hold are on the Arp page. Live step recording (REC on the Steps page) works with the arp on.
- **JAM SOUND:** Pluck and Acid pick the sound, Decay, Level.
- **JAM STEPS (the dots):** the pluck or acid line as sixteen dots. Pick a step with a white key, then set Type and Note. Press **REC** and play the keys in time to record a line. **SEL + REC** clears the line.
- **JAM STEPS 2:** Keep (stop the music changing your line), Clear, Range, Length.
- **JAM ARP, ARP 2, ARP 3:** hold a chord and the arp plays it. ARP 3 gives the arp its own sound.

## Save your work
Press **GLO + SAVE** together, then press **SAVE** again to store the piece.

## Settings (hold HOME)
Each settings page explains its knobs in plain words. **Saver**, **Advanced** (every page, Auto DJ and the scenes), **Big text** (the knob you turn fills the screen, for hard-to-see screens), **Scanlines**. Dial: next page.

## Simple by default
The dial walks only the pages most people use. Turn **Advanced** on for the envelope, LFO, drift, feel, phrase and master pages, and for Auto DJ and the scenes.

## If you are lost
HOME. Then hold a button to see what it does.
